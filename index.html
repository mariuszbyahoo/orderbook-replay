What you're looking at

      

        A replay of a real BTCUSDT order book (Binance L2 data: 41 min recorded 2026-11-24), drawn by hand with Canvas
        2D and SVG. Heatmap: resting liquidity by price over time (blue = bids, orange = asks). Ladder:
        the book at the playhead. Mid & spread: every value shown with its sample count and min/max. The
        top-right badge says how old the data on screen is and turns STALE when it is too old. The ◆ markers come
        from a deliberately simple, illustrative strategy, not a real one.
